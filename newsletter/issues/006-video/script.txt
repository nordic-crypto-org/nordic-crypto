[s1 @ 21.9s, story]
Binance says customers withdrew a net of about three point six four billion dollars on the first of November, after withdrawals reopened. E24 reported the exchange's figures.

[s2 @ 31.8s, story]
North Korea rejects the FBI's statement that it was behind the Binance theft. Aftenposten reports a statement calling the claim an absurd fabrication. That is North Korea's position, not a new finding.

[s3 @ 43.3s, story]
At Norwegian exchange Firi, trading the day after the hack was more than double a typical day, according to figures the firm shared with E24. The chief executive says it is too early to call it a lasting shift.

[s4 @ 54.4s, story]
Norges Bank sees no current need for a retail digital currency, but wants to be ready if that changes. Torbjørn Hægeland told a conference, Kaupr reports. A digital euro would not by itself decide it, he said.

[s5 @ 66.9s, story]
The same official said banks should prepare a tokenisation strategy, and that important settlement should use central bank money. Kaupr reported the speech.

[s6 @ 75.3s, story]
Norwegian exchange NBX has raised at least thirty-four million kroner from LDA Capital since 2022, by Kaupr's count of the company's own announcements. The latest issue closed on the third of November.

[s7 @ 86.8s, story]
Investornytt reports that Hyperliquid took in seven hundred and thirteen million dollars over twelve months, citing Bloomberg Businessweek. Founder Jeff Yan wants to reach US traders through regulated partners.

[b_se @ 97.7s, section - SWEDEN]
(music/stinger only)

[s8 @ 99.3s, story]
Safello reported a third-quarter operating profit of zero point eight million Swedish kronor, Kaupr reports from the company's figures. Revenue fell forty-one percent.

[s9 @ 108.7s, story]
Ulricehamns Tidning reports that one point five million Swedish kronor in crypto was transferred in a romance scam. The public excerpt does not establish that anyone has been charged or convicted.

[s10 @ 119.2s, story]
Virtune listed an AI crypto index ETP on the third of November, in Stockholm and on Xetra, Kaupr reports. Its sales chief called software agents a growth driver. That is the company's view.

[s11 @ 131.1s, story]
Tesla, SpaceX and others have objected to their shares being offered as tokens on OKXICE. Kryptovalutaguiden reported it. By CoinDesk's tally, the number of objections rose to fifty-six.

[s12 @ 142.3s, story]
Firi says the temporary lay-offs it announced in August will last until the end of March 2027. Chief executive Thuc Hoang told Kaupr that trading this year has been lower than planned.

[s13 @ 152.3s, story]
Sweden's Economic Crime Authority has charged two former Valuno executives with aggravated insider trading, Kaupr reports. No one has been convicted. The former chief executive denies wrongdoing, according to SvD.

[s14 @ 164.9s, story]
Goobit has signed an agreement with Kvarn Metals for gold in its planned Futureproof platform, the company told Kaupr. Goobit says the gold is backed one to one by physical gold.

Binance says the attackers used a fake job interview in September, then misused an internal tool on an engineer's laptop. DR reported the exchange's account, and that Binance says most of the losses have been covered.
