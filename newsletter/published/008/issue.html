The week’s crypto, bitcoin and blockchain stories from the Nordics that our editor has approved. Each item links to the story on Nordic Crypto.

Norway

Skatteetaten: 89,600 people reported crypto holdings of NOK 24.6 billion (original: Rekordmange rapporterer krypto i skattemeldingen)
According to preliminary figures from the Norwegian Tax Administration (Skatteetaten) for the 2025 income year, 89,600 people reported cryptocurrency holdings, with a total reported value of NOK 24.6 billion. Skatteetaten says checks are being tightened. From 2026, businesses that offer exchange and custody of cryptocurrency are required to provide information to Skatteetaten through third-party reporting.
Skatteetaten · 19 Nov 2026

Finanstilsynet: ESMA invites a hearing on tokenising collateral posted to CCPs (original: ESMA inviterer til høringsmøte om tokenisering av sikkerhet (collateral) som stilles til sentrale motparter (CCP))
The European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) has published a call for evidence on the tokenisation of collateral posted to central counterparties (CCPs) and is inviting a public hearing on 7 January 2027 from 09:30 to 17:00 at its premises at 201–203 rue de Bercy in Paris, and online, the Financial Supervisory Authority of Norway (Finanstilsynet) said on 18 November 2026. Registration closes on 16 December 2026, and written comments are due by 15 January 2027. The hearing and the call for evidence are set out on ESMA’s website.
Finanstilsynet · 18 Nov 2026

NBX applies for a bank licence in Latvia (original: NBX har levert søknad om bankkonsesjon i Latvia)
NBX has filed an application for a banking licence in Latvia for its wholly owned subsidiary, NBX Digital Asset Bank AS in Riga, according to a stock exchange notice reported by Kaupr on 17 November 2026. The application is for a specialised credit institution under Latvian law; it has been filed, not granted. Latvijas Banka will ask for more information before the application counts as complete, and the European Central Bank grants the licence on Latvijas Banka's recommendation. NBX says the planned digital bank would operate cross-border in Norway, Lithuania, Sweden, Finland, Poland and Germany, and that operations could start at the earliest by the end of 2027, with 2028 more likely.
Kaupr · 17 Nov 2026

H100 signs Coinbase Prime deal for custody, trading and financing of its bitcoin (original: Blir toppkunde hos kryptokjempe)
H100 Group AB has entered into a partnership with Coinbase for institutional custody, trading and financing of its bitcoin holdings through Coinbase Prime and Deribit, according to a company notice on 16 November 2026. The company says it holds about 3,506 bitcoin. On its own price basis of USD 64,600 on 15 November 2026, it valued those holdings at about USD 226 million, or SEK 2.3 billion. The notice describes a longer-term ambition to develop investor services; it does not say that an asset-management product has been launched.
Finansavisen · 16 Nov 2026 · may require a subscription

Sweden

Realtid: Briton sentenced to six years in Scattered Spider SIM-swap case (original: Flyttade telefonnummer till egna SIM-kort – döms till långa fängelsestraff)
A 24-year-old British man has been sentenced to six years in prison in the United States after pleading guilty to conspiracy to commit wire fraud and aggravated identity theft, as part of the group Scattered Spider, Realtid reported on 19 November 2026. According to Realtid, the group used SMS phishing and SIM swapping, and cryptocurrency worth about SEK 120 million was stolen; the paper, referring to the US Department of Justice, says at least twelve companies were affected. Realtid writes that he was arrested in Spain in May 2024 and extradited in April 2025, and that he is the fifth person sentenced in the US case. The official Justice Department text was not read for this report.
Realtid · 19 Nov 2026

H100 Group says bitcoin per share rose 4.36% in the third quarter (original: H100 økte bitcoin per aksje med 4,4 prosent i tredje kvartal)
In its interim report for 1 January to 30 September 2026, published on 19 November 2026, H100 Group says fully diluted bitcoin per share rose from 288.49 to 301.06 sats, an increase of 4.36%. The company's bitcoin holdings went from 1,051.03 BTC at the start of the quarter to 3,476.80 BTC at the end, after the acquisition of NSD, which took the holding to 3,506.4 BTC at completion, and a loss of about 30 BTC in an active pool of 233.42 BTC. Profit before tax was 142.9 million kronor, including a one-off gain of 171.0 million kronor from the acquisition; without that gain the result would have been negative. The report has not been reviewed by the company's auditors. The board has called an extraordinary general meeting for 7 December 2026 to seek authorisation to buy back shares; the company has not decided on a buyback, and that meeting has not yet been held.
Kaupr · 19 Nov 2026

Two people robbed of cryptocurrency in Tenhult, Swedish police say (original: Två personer bakbands och rånades på kryptovaluta)
Swedish police were called to central Tenhult at 03:35 on 18 November 2026 after two people were robbed of cryptocurrency in their home, according to a police summary for Jönköping County. The police say perpetrators entered the home, assaulted one of the victims and, under threat, forced them to transfer the currency by computer to another account, then bound the victims before leaving in an unknown direction. Nobody has been arrested. The incident is being investigated as aggravated robbery.
Omni · 18 Nov 2026

Huddlestock and Firi sign a conditional letter of intent on a tied-agent solution (original: Firi vil tilby aksjehandel til 430.000 kunder via Huddlestock)
Huddlestock Fintech AS has signed a letter of intent with the crypto exchange Firi for a tied-agent solution using Huddlestock's regulatory infrastructure, according to a stock-exchange notice published on 18 November 2026. The notice describes the arrangement as a letter of intent, not a completed agreement, and says the collaboration remains subject to further commercial, operational and regulatory assessment. Firi would, as planned, become a tied agent under Huddlestock's licence; the release describes Firi as the largest crypto exchange in the Nordics, with about 430,000 retail customers in Norway, Sweden and Denmark.
Kaupr · 18 Nov 2026

Malicious code served through Optimizely hit websites at about 178 organisations, including IKEA (original: Ikea drabbat i stor it-attack – ville stjäla krypto)
On 16 November 2026, malicious JavaScript was served through Optimizely Web Experimentation to visitors of websites at about 178 organisations, including IKEA, according to Report URI and security researcher Scott Helme. The code swapped cryptocurrency wallet addresses and skimmed payment cards, among other things. Report URI said about USD 7,040 had reached the attackers' wallets by 19:25 UTC on 16 November.
Omni · 17 Nov 2026

K33 agrees conditional SEK 13 million deal with Reverion Capital (original: K33 og 26.000 aksjonærer med Reverion-avtale verdt 13 MSEK)
K33 AB (publ) has entered into a conditional agreement with Reverion Capital AB (publ) worth SEK 13 million, according to a company notice on 16 November 2026. Subject to completion, about 26,000 K33 shareholders would receive Reverion shares worth SEK 5 million as a dividend in kind, and Reverion would issue an SEK 8 million interest-free, unsecured, subordinated convertible loan to K33. The deal requires approval at an extraordinary general meeting of K33 and sufficient free equity, and either party may terminate the agreement if the conditions have not been met by 31 December 2026.
Kaupr · 16 Nov 2026

Denmark

Lunar to sell its crypto customer portfolio to Pluto.markets and close Lunar Block (original: Lunar lukker kryptoforretning)
Lunar is selling its active crypto customer portfolio to the Danish investment platform Pluto.markets, the bank said in a press release on 16 November 2026. According to Børsen, Lunar is shutting down its crypto business, Lunar Block. Customers who hold crypto assets are to be transferred to Pluto.markets on 17 December 2026.
Børsen · 16 Nov 2026 · may require a subscription

Finland

Five EU foreign ministers ask for stronger action on crypto used to evade Russia sanctions (original: Valtonen vaatii EU:lta vahvempia toimia Venäjän sotaa rahoittavia kryptovirtoja vastaan)
The foreign ministers of Finland, Denmark, Estonia, France and Lithuania are asking the EU for stronger action against the use of crypto-assets to evade Russia sanctions, the Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs said on 16 November 2026. The ministers want the EU to target crypto networks that help sustain Russia’s war economy, tighten enforcement in member states and close regulatory and supervisory gaps. The letter itself has not been published in full.
salkunrakentaja · 16 Nov 2026

Iceland

Icelandic host 1984 temporarily shuts all services after a serious attack (original: Kennir 1984 um tveggja milljarða króna rafmyntasvik)
The Icelandic hosting company 1984 ehf. temporarily shut down all of its services after a serious cyber attack, Vísir reported on 20 November 2026. The company said the systems were closed deliberately to stop the attack, and that it does not know whether any data were stolen. Wagyu, the issuer of the XMR1 token, claims that an issuing key stored on a deleted server at 1984 was leaked; that claim is unconfirmed.
Vísir · 20 Nov 2026
